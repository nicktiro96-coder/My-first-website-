📖 How to Play Rules

1. Game Cost: Each spin costs exactly 1 Pula, deducted from your main balance.

2. Base Reel Wins:

  • Matching Pair: Wins a random payout up to 17 Pula.
  • 3-of-a-Kind: Wins a large random payout up to 1000 Pula!
  • ⭐ Wild Stars: Substitute for any fruit or number symbol to help complete winning pairs or lines.

3. The 3 Lion Bonus Pots:

When a gold Coin Chip (🪙) lands on the reels, a random Lion Head swallows it and grows larger. When a Lion head grows big enough, it bursts open to trigger its feature:

  • 1️⃣ Red Lion: Triggers 24 Free Spins where all wins are automatically multiplied by 2x.
  • 2️⃣ Blue Lion: Triggers the Mystery Pick'em game where you smash 3 pots for random cash rewards.
  • 3️⃣ Gold Lion: Triggers the Pin2Win Respin Engine. Fill the entire 3x3 board with coins to burst the ultimate 5,000 Pula Grand Jackpot!
